Notes that are easy to group and link.

Nicotes sits between OneNote and Obsidian: notebooks and pages when you want structure, links and a graph when you want connections. One Docker container on your own server, and your notes stay yours.

Get started Take the tour

Everything a notebook needs, nothing that gets in the way

Start writing right away. Structure and connections grow as you go.

Notebooks and subpages

Group pages into colour-coded notebooks and nest them as deep as you like. Drag to reorder, tag anything, find it all with Ctrl K.

Links, backlinks, graph

Type [[ to link pages. Every page shows what links to it and which pages mention it without a link yet.

One list for every task

Every checkbox from every page lands in Tasks, sorted by due date. Ticking one off there updates its page.

Pages that know what they are

A recipe gets servings and timers, a list becomes a checklist, a how-to turns into a step-by-step guide, a story is read aloud.

AI, only if you want it

Bring an OpenRouter or Routeway key to ask your notes, research the web with sources, or let the assistant suggest edits you approve one by one.

Safe on your own server

Daily backups with retention, version history for every page, and an export that opens as an Obsidian vault.

Graph

See how your ideas connect

The graph shows pages joined by links, subpages and tags, coloured by notebook. Filter by notebook or tag, or focus on one page and its neighbours. Each page also has a small graph of its own.

Interactive views

Cook from your notes, not from your phone's tiny text

Switch a page to View and Nicotes lays it out the way it's meant to be used: scale a recipe, start timers right from the steps, and follow along in a full-screen cook mode. Whatever you change there edits the page itself.

Tasks and journal

Write tasks where they belong

Jot a - [ ] into any page, add a date if it has one, and it shows up under Tasks: overdue, today, the next days. The daily journal note is one click away and takes new tasks too.

In your pocket, too

Install Nicotes on your phone's home screen. On Android, links shared to it become notes.

Browser extension

Nicotes next to every page you read

The Nicotes extension opens your notes in the sidebar of Firefox, or the side panel of Chrome and Edge, so you can take notes while you read. Enter your Nicotes address once and sign in; the Firefox version keeps itself up to date.

Your server needs NICOTES_BROWSER_EXTENSION=true in its .env.

Add to Firefox All downloads
  1. Download the extension from the latest release.
  2. Firefox: open the signed .xpi. Chrome / Edge: unpack the zip and load it under chrome://extensions with developer mode on.
  3. Click the Nicotes button in the toolbar and enter your address.

Up and running in a minute

All you need is Docker. Download two files, choose a username and password, start it.

Terminal 
mkdir nicotes && cd nicotes
curl -fsSLO https://git.nico.software/1n9i9c7om/nicotes/-/raw/main/docker-compose.yml
curl -fsSL https://git.nico.software/1n9i9c7om/nicotes/-/raw/main/.env.example -o .env
nano .env   # choose NICOTES_ADMIN_USER and your own NICOTES_ADMIN_PASSWORD
docker compose up -d

Then open http://localhost:3000. Updates are a docker compose pull && docker compose up -d away. For your phone, run it behind a reverse proxy with HTTPS. The README covers backups, AI keys, the extension and everything else.
